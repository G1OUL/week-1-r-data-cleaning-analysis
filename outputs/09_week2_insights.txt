LINE CHART DESIGN DECISION
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A conventional time-series line chart was not produced because the UCI Adult
dataset records a single cross-sectional survey snapshot (1994) and does not
contain a temporal or naturally ordered continuous progression variable.
Using a line chart without valid time-based or ordered continuous X-axis data
would produce a misleading visual that implies change over time when none exists.
Instead, Figure 13 presents an ordered age-cohort trend line using cross-sectional
brackets, which is analytically justified as an ordered categorical axis and is
clearly labelled as a cohort comparison rather than a time series.
